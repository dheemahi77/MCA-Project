About EMD-BPNN Training

The EMD-BPNN model trains separate neural networks for each component:

  • High Frequency Component: Captures short-term market fluctuations
  • Low Frequency Component: Models medium-term market cycles
  • Trend Component: Forecasts the long-term price direction

A final combined model integrates predictions from all components to generate the final forecast.
