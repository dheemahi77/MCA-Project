About EMD Decomposition

Empirical Mode Decomposition (EMD) is an adaptive time-frequency data analysis method that decomposes a signal into Intrinsic Mode Functions (IMFs).

Benefits of EMD decomposition in oil price forecasting:

  • Isolates high-frequency market fluctuations from longer-term trends
  • Handles non-linear and non-stationary time series
  • Improves forecasting by modeling each component separately
  • Provides insight into underlying market dynamics
