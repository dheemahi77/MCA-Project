Model Insights

BPNN Model

Standard backpropagation neural network that processes the raw time series data directly. Simple but effective for short-term forecasting.

  • Simple architecture
  • Faster training time
  • Better for stable market conditions

EMD-BPNN Model

Hybrid model that decomposes the time series into components first, then applies separate neural networks to each component.

  • Advanced component modeling
  • Better handles market volatility
  • Superior for complex price patterns

Understanding Performance Metrics

The models are evaluated using several key metrics:

  • RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): Measures the average magnitude of forecast errors. Lower values indicate better accuracy.
  • MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error): Expresses error as a percentage of the actual values. Lower values indicate better accuracy.
  • Direction Accuracy (%): The percentage of times the model correctly predicts the price movement direction. Higher values indicate better trend forecasting.

For oil price forecasting, direction accuracy is often considered the most important metric for trading applications, while RMSE and MAPE are crucial for budget planning and risk management.
