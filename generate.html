About Synthetic Data Generation

This tool creates realistic synthetic oil price data with configurable parameters:

  • Start/End Date: The time period for the generated data
  • Volatility: Controls the day-to-day price variability
  • Trend: Sets the overall upward or downward price direction

The generated data follows a geometric Brownian motion model with the specified parameters, creating realistic price movements that mimic real market behavior.
