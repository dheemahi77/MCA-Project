About The Technology

EMD-BPNN Hybrid Model

This system combines Empirical Mode Decomposition (EMD) with Back Propagation Neural Networks (BPNN) to create a powerful hybrid forecasting model. By decomposing complex oil price data into simpler components, the system can identify both high-frequency market fluctuations and long-term trends.

The EMD-BPNN approach has demonstrated superior accuracy compared to traditional forecasting methods, with improved performance in both directional accuracy and error metrics.
